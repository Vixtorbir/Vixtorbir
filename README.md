## Víctor Cárdenas

**Gameplay Programmer** · Unity / C# · C++ · Barcelona

I'm a final-year student of Game Design & Development at CITM (Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya).
What I enjoy most is the part of a game you feel in your hands: responsive controls, combat that reads
clearly, and the feedback (hitstop, camera, sound) that makes every action land. Audio is my second
interest: I like connecting gameplay and sound so that the two work as one system.

I've worked both on my own and in teams. At university I was lead programmer on a five-person project,
and I was part of the team that built our own C++ engine and shipped a game on top of it. On my own
I enjoy taking a game from the first prototype to something people can download and play.

I'm currently looking for a **gameplay programming internship** (university agreement), in Barcelona
or anywhere I can relocate to.
