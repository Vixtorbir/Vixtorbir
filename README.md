## About me

I'm a game developer from Barcelona. I mostly work in Unity and C#, and I enjoy gameplay and combat: responsive controls, hits that read clearly and the feedback that makes them land.

- **On my own** I shipped an Android game and wrote the combat core of a 2.5D fighting game.
- **In teams** I was lead programmer in a team of 5, and I worked on two C++ engines and on a game made with one of them.
- **Audio** is my second strong point: I integrated FMOD into a C++ engine and did the audio of a Unity walking simulator.

> **I'm looking for an internship at a game studio, in any role** (university agreement, starting before February 2027). Barcelona, or anywhere I can relocate to.
